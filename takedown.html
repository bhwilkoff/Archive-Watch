Removal Requests

Archive Watch hosts no video. Every film plays from the Internet Archive, as its uploader published it there, and Archive Watch shows only titles its rights audit keeps.

To remove a file from the Internet Archive

The file is the Internet Archive's to remove. See its rights and removal help. Once a file is gone from the Internet Archive, it is gone from Archive Watch too.

To remove something from Archive Watch

This form covers anything Archive Watch shows: a film, a series, an episode or a single file. What is removed comes off every Archive Watch app and this website, including search, shares and every feed, and stays off.
