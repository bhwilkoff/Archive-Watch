About & Attribution

✕
Archive Watch A cinematheque for the Internet Archive

Feature films, classic TV, newsreels, silent cinema, animation, ephemeral film — every title is public domain or otherwise free to share. No account, no tracking; your favorites and progress stay in this browser.

Sources & attribution

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Posters, backdrops, cast, and synopses come from The Movie Database (TMDb), with Wikidata, Wikimedia Commons, and the Library of Congress as fallbacks. Films, television, and ephemera are served by the Internet Archive.

Support the Internet Archive

Archive Watch is free, with no ads and no account. If it earns a place in your evenings, the library it draws from runs on donations.

Get the app

Archive Watch is also a native app — on Apple TV, iPhone, and iPad, on Android and Google TV, on Fire TV, and on Roku. Same catalog, lean-back living-room playback, and (on phones) Clip Studio for making shareable clips.

Watch Together on YouTube and Twitch

From the Mac, iPhone or Apple TV app, host a live watch-along of a public-domain film on your own YouTube or Twitch channel — with your camera, your microphone, and the friends on your call watching in step. It uses YouTube API Services; see the Privacy Policy and Terms.
