About

Archive Watch turns the Internet Archive's public-domain moving-image collection — feature films, classic TV, newsreels, silent cinema, animation, ephemeral film — into a cinematheque. Every title is public domain or otherwise free to share. No account, no tracking; your favorites and progress stay in this browser.

Sources & attribution

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Posters, backdrops, cast, and synopses come from The Movie Database (TMDb), with Wikidata, Wikimedia Commons, and the Library of Congress as fallbacks. Films, television, and ephemera are served by the Internet Archive.
